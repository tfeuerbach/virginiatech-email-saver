About this service

VT Email Saver is a live service operated by Thomas Feuerbach. It schedules automated logins so Virginia Tech Gmail accounts stay active. Optional SMS messages are sent only by VT Email Saver — not by Virginia Tech or any third-party platform.

Enable text messages

After you create an account, open your dashboard and go to Notifications → Text Message. Enter your phone number, check the consent box below (unchecked by default), and click Save. SMS is optional — the core service works without it.

Notifications

SMS text messages are optional. Email reminders are enabled by default. Enable text messages only if you want Duo codes sent to your phone.

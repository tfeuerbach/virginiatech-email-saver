Service

VT Email Saver is operated by Thomas Feuerbach and provides automated Virginia Tech Gmail login scheduling to help users keep their accounts active. Thomas Feuerbach is the sole operator and the only sender of optional SMS account notifications. The service is available at hokiesaver.tfeuerbach.dev.

Account responsibility

You are responsible for the accuracy of credentials you submit and for approving Duo authentication when prompted. You may delete your account and all stored data at any time from your dashboard.

SMS notifications

SMS is optional. If you opt in, you agree to receive automated text messages from VT Email Saver related to your account, including Duo verification codes needed during scheduled logins. You can opt out by replying STOP or disabling SMS in your dashboard.

No marketing

VT Email Saver does not send marketing or promotional text messages.

Disclaimer

The service is provided as-is. Automated logins depend on Virginia Tech systems remaining available and may fail for reasons outside our control.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 14, 2026

What this is

I built VT Email Saver as an open-source tool to help Virginia Tech students and alumni keep their university Gmail accounts from being deactivated. Every line of code is on GitHub — you can audit it yourself.

What data I collect

  • VT email address — used to identify your account and log in on your behalf.
  • VT password — encrypted with AWS KMS before it touches the database. The plaintext is never written to disk or logged.
  • Phone number (optional) — only stored if you opt in to SMS reminders. Shared with Twilio solely for delivering text messages.
  • Notification email (optional) — only stored if you choose to receive reminders at a different address.

How I use your data

  • Periodically log in to your VT Gmail via an automated browser session to prevent account deactivation.
  • Send you reminders (email and/or SMS) before each scheduled login so you can approve the Duo 2FA push.

I do not read, store, or forward any of your emails. The automated session logs in and immediately ends.

SMS notifications

  • You may opt in to receive automated SMS login reminders from VT Email Saver.
  • Messages are sent approximately 1 minute before each scheduled login attempt.
  • Message frequency varies based on your chosen login cadence ({{ min_cadence }}–{{ max_cadence }} messages per {{ max_cadence }}-day period).
  • Standard message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time.
  • Your phone number is shared with Twilio for message delivery and is not used for any other purpose.

Data security

  • Credentials are encrypted using AWS Key Management Service (KMS). Encryption keys live in Amazon's hardware security modules and never leave AWS.
  • All traffic is served over HTTPS via Cloudflare.
  • Session cookies are HTTP-only and same-site restricted.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained only while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the dashboard. When you do, your encrypted credentials, phone number, and notification preferences are permanently removed from the database.

Third-party services

  • AWS KMS — credential encryption
  • AWS SES — email delivery
  • Twilio — SMS delivery (only if you opt in)
  • Cloudflare — DNS and tunnel

I don't use any analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising services.

Contact

Questions, concerns, or feedback? Feel free to open an issue on GitHub.

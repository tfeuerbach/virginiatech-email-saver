Zero plaintext storage

Your password is encrypted with AWS KMS before it touches our database. The encryption key lives in Amazon's hardware security modules — never on this server.

Automated & hands-off

A background scheduler checks daily for accounts due for login. It decrypts your credentials only at login time, drives a headless Chrome browser through VT's SSO + Duo 2FA, then discards the plaintext.

You stay in the loop

Get an email, text, or calendar reminder before each login so you're ready to enter your Duo verification code. Toggle notifications on or off from your dashboard.

About this service

A live tool operated by Thomas Feuerbach

VT Email Saver helps Virginia Tech students and alumni keep their university Gmail accounts from being deactivated by scheduling automated logins on a cadence you choose. It is a fully launched service at hokiesaver.tfeuerbach.dev.
